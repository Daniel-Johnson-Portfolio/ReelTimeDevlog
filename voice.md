# Devlog voice

Paste two or three paragraphs of your own writing here (an old post, a long message, anything
in your natural voice). The devlog session copies the tone, not the content.

## Defaults until then
- First person, as the human ("I asked the Director for...").
- Short paragraphs. Specific over impressive: what broke, how long it took, what changed.
- Credit the crew by role when they did the work ("the UI session rebuilt the menu from the mockup").
- One idea per post. A clip or screenshot for anything visual.

## The human's preferences (11 Nov)
- Plain voice is fine (a writing sample may follow). Posts may use the human's name; first person "I".
- Visual first: nice button UI, images and video wherever possible; lead with media, keep text short.
- Framing: the film's production diary (Dailies, Behind the scenes, Bloopers, Milestones). Full brief: $COORD_DIR/devlog/brief.md (private, never in this repo).
