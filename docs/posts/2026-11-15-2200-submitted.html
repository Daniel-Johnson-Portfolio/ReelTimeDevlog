Submitted at 22:10 UK, Sunday 15 November, 3 hours 49 minutes before the 01:59 deadline. The yellow marker on the timeline shows where.

What went in: the Windows Shipping build of game commit 0ad3f1e62, a 456 MB zip, checksummed and frozen at 21:27, with the 60-second gameplay video above. I entered it solo, as a student, in four categories: Solo Driver, Glass Box (this devlog), Render Royalty, and the NVIDIA RTX award. That last one is for DLSS, which meant porting NVIDIA's plugin from Unreal 5.8 to 5.9.

The last day

Sunday was the day I played and the crew fixed. Six playtests, each one a list (09:40, 12:10, 13:50, 16:40, 19:10, 20:25), and the crew turned every list into tasks, a merge window and a package. The rules for the day were mine: "what is in the game now is our work adding is risky."

  • Round one, the quit button at boot, round two, round three
  • Content lock at 16:01
  • The final build in Shipping, and the final build in a browser

After the content lock, my last two playtests found three more things: the saucer could kill its own aliens, right stick down looked up, and the Settings menu skipped a row on a controller. The first two are fixed. The third fix was abandoned at 21:25 after a memory emergency and three failed attempts, so it ships as a known flaw.

The known flaw: in Settings, on a controller, pressing down from Stick look jumps past Invert Look to FOV. The fix needed WBP_Settings rebuilt. At 20:44 that rebuild pushed the editor to 49 GB of memory and filled the C: drive, and I closed the editor by hand. After that the widget wouldn't recompile in a fresh editor, three ways in a row, and the Director abandoned the fix at 21:22. It's not in this build. It's first on the list if we carry on.

Play it

  • Windows: the jam entry, on its itch.io page. The link goes here once the entries are public.
  • In your browser: Play In Browser. Unreal Engine 5.9's new web export running the build from one round earlier (3f07edb57). Very experimental, Chrome only.

What's next for this site

Nothing new gets added to the game now. This devlog stays up as it is, and anything written after the deadline is labelled "After the jam". A snapshot of the whole site goes up as a download next to the game, so it can still be read if the site ever goes down.
