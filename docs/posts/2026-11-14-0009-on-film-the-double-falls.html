On film: the cardboard double falls, and the spitter spits

Two of tonight's answers to playtest three finally made it onto film at 23:30, on the third try for one of them: the stunt double cutout in the screen test, and the spitter zombie's lob.

Day 2, Fri 13 NovHour 4400:09

  • Gameplay
  • NPC AI
  • World
  • Audio
  • Engine

Both were built within ninety minutes of my "too easy" note (what changed). Getting them on camera took longer.

The cardboard stunt double

At 19:30 the engine's script couldn't move the player, so the cutout never came into frame. At 23:30 the autoplayer walked the whole screen test instead, prompt by prompt. The director's notes, as they appear on screen:

"Now stand still. See how the world waits for you?" "Hit the stunt double. It's cardboard, it won't mind." "Out of rounds? Put it down. The glow points you to the next one." "That's a wrap on the screen test. You've got the part."

The same run shows what still needs work: the bot stared at a wall for twenty seconds where the wall kick should be, then stood on the fallen cutout getting hit by prop bullets ("Ouch. Again."). That's the bot, not the tutorial, but it's on the list.

The spitter

This one took three tries. At 19:30 the camera never caught a lob. At 21:30 the shot failed for a reason that only makes sense in this game: the camera stood still in front of the spitter, so time stood still too, and it never spat. At 23:30 the script strafed left and right the whole time to keep the clock running, and the spitter lobbed five times in sixteen seconds.

Still to come: the acid colour on the glob, and a shot where you can see it land.

Round three: a gunman in the screen test, and a saucer that owns its kills

My longest note of the day, at 13:50, became six tasks; by 15:12 the tutorial had a noir gunman instead of a bullet-dodge drill, the saucer warned you and signed its kills, and retakes stopped adding stars (I'd scored 25 out of 24).

BloopersDay 4, hour 81

Round two: held Space, a two-hit machete and faster enemies

My 12:10 playtest found that holding Space to skip also pressed the next button, that the machete killed in one hit, and that enemies were slow to react; all four fixes were in a package that passed 25 journey steps at 13:00.

BloopersDay 4, hour 79

Final day, round one: fixes only

I played the overnight build at 09:40 and wrote one long note; by 10:57 five sessions had fixed all of it, from missing tutorial lines to a death screen you can actually read, and the build went green.
