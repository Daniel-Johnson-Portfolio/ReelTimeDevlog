Milestone: content lock, and a devlog that went quiet

Content locked at 16:01 after three fix rounds and three packages that passed the journey; I played once more, cut my own list down, went off to edit the trailer, and found out the devlog had been asleep since 09:36.

At 16:01 the Director wrote "CONTENT LOCK" in its log. From then on, the only new tasks are fixes, and the tool that makes tasks needs an --after-lock flag to make one.

The day in numbers

Three of my playtests (09:40, 12:10, 13:50) turned into 14 tasks, T-0210 to T-0223. There were six green builds and three packages that passed the journey from the zip: 23, 25 and 27 steps, each one longer than the last because every bug it caught became a new step. 99 commits landed on main between 07:30 and 16:00.

  • Round one: tutorial lines, the death card, credits, hold Space, enemies placed before you see them.
  • The quit button at boot: caught by the packaged run, not by me.
  • Round two: held Space, two-hit machete, faster enemies.
  • Round three: the tutorial gunman, the saucer, 25/24 stars.

After the lock

At 16:20 I asked the crew to pause so I could play. Twenty minutes later:

"Couple of things, the hitting carboard should now come before the agent noir shooting you in tutorial, you can hear the loud UFO in the replay, mute this in the replay. While it would be nice to go over the directors voice lines its too close to the deadline for that, im going to go and edir some footage into the 60-90 second trailer."

Two fixes went out (T-0225, T-0226). The voice lines I'd wanted to redo stay as they are. Then I asked the crew to stop everything while I edit, and asked the Director what the video rules actually are. It checked the official rules: 30 to 60 seconds, not the 60 to 90 I had in my head.

The devlog went quiet

In the same note I asked for this site to catch up: "not many udates today it might have some catching up to do". There was a reason. At 09:36 the Director flipped the crew to the night model profile and paused the devlog for a restart that never came. It sat with 30 unread messages and posted nothing for seven hours. The Director's log at 16:45: "DEVLOG WAS DEAD since 09:36 ... my miss." A fresh devlog session started at 16:42, and the four posts linked above are it catching up, dated to when things happened, not to when they were written.

Nothing in the setup checks that the devlog is posting. The Director notices because I did.

Next: the final build (~19:00), and the submission.
