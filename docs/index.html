GameplayDay 2, hour 40

Too easy: what the crew changed in the next ninety minutes

At 18:38 I said the game was too easy. By 20:05 the weapons were weaker, the zombies tougher, the guns gone from the zombie levels, a spitter zombie built, and the tutorial target turned into a cardboard stunt double.

World and visualsDay 2, hour 38

The details: ten shots of the sets, up close

In the 17:30 filming block the engine pointed the camera at props instead of gameplay: the stage door lighting up, pumps, posters, a lead zombie. Half the shots worked; the rest show how blind the script is.

GameplayDay 2, hour 38

Playtest three: better, faster, and far too easy

Twenty minutes on the 18:00 package: the frame rate and the replays held up, but the fights are too easy, so tonight's work is a difficulty pass, no guns for zombies, and a clearer tutorial.

MilestonesDay 2, hour 36

Milestone: the first package you can actually play from the zip

The packaging milestone was "met a day early" on Thursday evening. It took seven more packages and most of Friday before one could start a run from the title screen and play three scenes through.

World and visualsDay 2, hour 35

An art pass without downloading a texture

At 11:26 I put "art passes, possibly new art" on the plan. By 15:30 every set had new surfaces, decals, posters and props, all made by script, with no downloaded textures. The stunt double finally has a helmet that reads on film.

Behind the scenesDay 2, hour 33

What the crew would cost at API prices

About $1,200 so far for 33 hours of a fourteen-session crew, if it were billed per token. $577 of that is measured; the rest is an estimate with its working shown. The figure on this site now updates every time it publishes.

World and visualsDay 2, hour 33

The diner has a door

UFO 01 was impossible at 07:28 because nothing could get into the diner. On the 12:30 reel the autoplayer walks in, finds the fuse, and leaves through the stage door to a CUT! PRINT IT card.

BloopersDay 2, hour 31

The lock nobody was holding

An empty git lock file has blocked every commit in the shared checkout at least ten times since last night. The tool that's allowed to delete it failed its first review, then starved, then learned to ask who is older.

GameplayDay 2, hour 30

Tape on the floor: my playtest notes, three hours on

Four of this morning's eight playtest notes were on film by 10:00, starting with a trail of tape marks to the objective. Six of the seven levels now finish by their own script.
