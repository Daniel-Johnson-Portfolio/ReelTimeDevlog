Characters and NPCs

The characters you meet: their behaviour, animation and personality.

6 posts

GameplayDay 3, hour 60

Standing still stops being safe

In a game where time slows when you stop, stopping was the winning move. The Enemies session added idle pressure, the first film of it failed on the real levels, and the 14:45 reel shows a player who stands still dying in every level filmed.

GameplayDay 2, hour 44

On film: the cardboard double falls, and the spitter spits

Two of tonight's answers to playtest three finally made it onto film at 23:30, on the third try for one of them: the stunt double cutout in the screen test, and the spitter zombie's lob.

World and visualsDay 2, hour 35

An art pass without downloading a texture

At 11:26 I put "art passes, possibly new art" on the plan. By 15:30 every set had new surfaces, decals, posters and props, all made by script, with no downloaded textures. The stunt double finally has a helmet that reads on film.

GameplayDay 1, hour 19

The machete hit, now the zombie feels it

After the first playtest I said the zombies didn't seem to feel a hit; by the 23:30 reel a new feel session had added a freeze on contact, a flash, a camera shove, a flinch and blood.

Characters and NPCsDay 1, hour 9

The cast: five bodies, built by script in under half an hour

Every enemy was Unreal's grey mannequin this morning. The NPC session built three zombies, a gangster and an alien with MakeHuman in headless Blender, all on the mannequin's skeleton.

Characters and NPCsDay 1, hour 3

The first zombie walks, lunges and ragdolls

The NPC session's shambler walks at the camera, shows a tell, lunges, and ragdolls from one hit. Its two faster cousins stood still in their own capture.
