GameplayDay 1, hour 13

Bullets you can watch: the ray gun and the revolver in slow time

Each film has its own gun. Shots are real objects in the world, so when you stand still they hang in the air with everything else.

GameplayDay 1, hour 12

The Director's Cut: the replay finally shows up on film

Four sets of reels failed to show a full take with its replay, the point of the game. The fifth does. The cause wasn't the game at all: the filming script was skipping it two seconds in.

InterfaceDay 1, hour 8

An hour later: a slate, a HUD and ACTION!

At 11:15 no level had a HUD. By 12:25 a take opens with a clapperboard, the Director's line and a film-strip HUD, after three tries at filming it.
