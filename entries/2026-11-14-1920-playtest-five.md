The [crash at 17:27](2026-11-14-1745-every-tab-gone.html) pushed tonight's package back. At 18:16 I asked the Director:

> "Think we can try and get a Playtest ready in the next 40 minutes?"

A package couldn't make it, so I played the one from 12:38 while the engine got the new build green. At 18:56 I typed one long note. In pieces:

**The replay**
- "Replay is hard to follow. New cameras are great but cutting between the kills makes it hard to understand, would honestly be better to just show the whole replay."
- "Stunt double's animation can clip through floor when sliding on replay, stunt double's character model could do with an improvement pass."

**Enemies**
- "AI path finding is bugging out when the enemy is hit, sometimes they walk in odd directions."
- "In the Dead Mile scene 5 room 9 there is a spitter behind a big enemy at the end. Please separate these and make more user clarity on when the spitter is spitting."
- "The red arrows for the range incoming fire need to be more clear, maybe flashing and such."

@video media/incoming-fire-arrows-noir.mp4 "Two gunmen at 10 metres on the noir set. The red triangles at the screen edges are the incoming-fire warning: small, under a second each, and the left one sits over the script page. The orange labels over the gunmen are test read-outs, not in the game."

**Pickups and props**
- "No more walking over to pick up unless it's ammo for the same currently equipped weapon, instead show press E prompt."
- "Need to make it a step clearer that the barrels and gas pumps can explode."
- "Tutorial is quite dark and the cardboard is still in the air."

The last one is partly an old-build note. The screen test got new lights in the build that went green at 19:05, and in the engine's 19:15 stills the cut-out stands on the floor. The room is only a little brighter though, so that task stays open.

## Four minutes

The Director had eight tasks out by 19:00, T-0195 to T-0202, across eight sessions. At 19:10 the gameplay-2 session reported the replay rewritten: one continuous shot, fast-forward capped at four times speed, and the double no longer sinks into the floor on a slide. It's coded. The build and the film come next.

## The last night like this

The same note ended with this:

> "We are slowly approaching the end of the usage I have for this jam. It isn't alarming yet, we are all good, but tomorrow won't be as automated: it will be me playing and you fixing very particular things and requests for dev log."

So tonight is the crew's last long stretch on its own. The Director's order for it: every note above fixed, shown on film and in a package by morning, and nothing speculative.

## Update, 23:08: a quick play of the 21:00 build

I played the 21:00 package before bed. My note to the Director:

> "Things are better in that one, pathfinding still broken. Also it looks like there is some sort of scene description at the start of each scene, however the UI for it is overlapped by the clapper board and the objective screen."

Two new top-priority tasks went out straight away: T-0208, pathfinding, to the npc-ai session, and T-0209, the scene card overlap, to the ui session. Then the crew switched to night mode.
