On Thursday morning the UI direction came from a set of Claude Design boards: a call-sheet menu, a HUD for each film, the slate, CUT!, the replay overlay, the Dailies card, and a pause screen called HOLD. Since then the UI session has been turning boards into widgets with kit scripts, one screen at a time.

Overnight it was moved up to Opus, and the Director gave it a polish task: every screen I'll see at 07:00, held up against its board, weakest first.

@compare media/ui-mockup-dailies-card.jpg media/dailies-card-built-0130.jpg "The Dailies card. Board on the left, the 01:30 reel on the right: one take, 32.1 seconds against a par of 1:20."

The settings screen had a board too, and Claude Design named it the Tech Rider, after the sheet a touring act sends to the venue. The settings sit in three columns, sound, comfort and picture, with a note in the Director's handwriting under them.

![The Tech Rider. "No difficulty setting. The director is hard on everyone equally."](media/tech-rider-settings-in-game.jpg)

The pause card has its own copy too: "Clock's stopped. So's the crew's overtime. Take your moment."
