**Submitted at SUBMIT_TIME UK, Sunday 15 November.** The yellow marker on the [timeline](../timeline.html) shows where.

What went in: the Windows **Shipping** build of game commit **0ad3f1e62**, a 456 MB zip, checksummed and frozen at 21:27.

## The last day

Sunday was the day I played and the crew fixed. Six playtests, each one a list (09:40, 12:10, 13:50, 16:40, 19:10, 20:25), and the crew turned every list into tasks, a merge window and a package. The rules for the day were mine: "what is in the game now is our work adding is risky."

- [Round one](2026-11-15-1100-final-day-fixes-only.html), [the quit button at boot](2026-11-15-1110-the-quit-button-at-boot.html), [round two](2026-11-15-1300-round-two-held-space.html), [round three](2026-11-15-1515-round-three-gunman-and-saucer.html)
- [Content lock](2026-11-15-1600-content-lock.html) at 16:01
- [The final build in Shipping](2026-11-15-1815-final-build-shipping.html), and [the final build in a browser](2026-11-15-1900-play-it-in-your-browser.html)

After the content lock, my last two playtests found three more things: the saucer could kill its own aliens, right stick down looked up, and the Settings menu skipped a row on a controller. The first two are fixed. The third fix [was abandoned](2026-11-15-1815-final-build-shipping.html) at 21:25 after a memory emergency and three failed attempts, so it ships as a known flaw.
